How Grace works

Structured enough to help you move forward. Human enough to make room for complexity.

Grace’s style is collaborative, culturally responsive, trauma-informed, and emotionally attuned. Sessions can move between reflection, emotional processing, and practical skill-building depending on what you need.

1

Notice emotional patterns and what may be happening underneath them.

Make sense of the larger context.

About Grace

Clinical depth shaped by work across outpatient, intensive, and cross-cultural settings.

Grace Yin is a Licensed Mental Health Counselor in Indiana and a Licensed Professional Counselor in Wisconsin, and is registered as an out-of-state telehealth provider in Florida. She earned her M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling from Marquette University and her B.S. in Applied Psychology from East China Normal University.

Her background includes individual and group therapy, intake and risk assessment, crisis intervention, DBT groups, CBT education, and work in intensive outpatient and partial hospitalization settings. She integrates evidence-based approaches including DBT and Cognitive Processing Therapy while adapting the work to each client’s goals and readiness.

Frequently asked questions

Before you reach out.

Does Grace provide therapy in Chinese?

Yes. Grace provides therapy in English, Mandarin Chinese, and Shanghainese.

Can I work with Grace if I am in Indiana, Wisconsin, or Florida?

Grace provides telehealth therapy to clients located in Indiana and Wisconsin and through her Florida out-of-state telehealth registration.

What concerns does Grace work with?

Her listed clinical specialties include ADHD, anxiety, depression, bipolar disorder, stress management, cross-cultural identity, self-esteem, life transitions, trauma and PTSD, and relationship issues.

What does therapy with Grace feel like?

Her style is structured, collaborative, culturally responsive, and emotionally attuned. Some sessions may focus on emotional processing; others may emphasize concrete strategies for stress, regulation, or daily functioning.

Yes. Her current Mind X Associates profile indicates that she is accepting new clients. Appointment availability can change.
